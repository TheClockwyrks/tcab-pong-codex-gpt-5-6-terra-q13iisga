# Carom

Carom turns a classic paddle rally into a compact bank-shot duel. One bright ball
crosses a dark arena between two vivid paddles and a pair of amber obstacles.

Every rally opens with a quick serve, then becomes a game of angles. Hit near a
paddle edge to send the ball away sharply, or use the center rails to make an
unexpected return.

The signature is spin: move your paddle as the ball connects and its flight
bends through open space. A small adjustment can turn a safe return into a
curving winner.

Play against the reactive solo opponent or share the keyboard for a local first-
to-eleven duel.
